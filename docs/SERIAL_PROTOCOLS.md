# Serial Protocols & Framing

## Common Configurations
- **8N1**: 8 Data Bits, No Parity, 1 Stop Bit (Most common default).
- **7E1**: 7 Data Bits, Even Parity, 1 Stop Bit (Common in industrial telecommunications).

## Serial BREAK
A serial BREAK condition occurs when the TX line is held at logic low (spacing state) longer than a full frame time.
